# Lab Report: Requirements Engineering with AI

## 1. Introduction
This report documents the requirements engineering artefacts generated for the Smart Campus Study Room Booking system using AI assistance under Level D guidelines.

## 2. Scenario Overview
The system facilitates study room reservations for students and administrative management for facility managers, governed by specific business rules and explicit out-of-scope bounds.

## 3. User Stories Generation & Review
Generated structured user stories based on the fixed prompt template. Each story was reviewed against INVEST criteria and assigned unique IDs, priorities, and explicit assumptions.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria
Detailed Given-When-Then acceptance criteria were written and audited for core functional workflows.

## 5. Use Case Diagram
A PlantUML use-case diagram (`use-cases.puml`) was created illustrating interactions between the actors and the required use cases.

## 6. Traceability Matrix
Mapped business rules, user stories, and acceptance criteria in `traceability.md` to guarantee full coverage of requirements.

## 7. AI Collaboration Reflection
Using AI accelerated the initial artifact drafting phase significantly. However, manual review was essential to align edge cases, verify business rules, and ensure strict formatting compliance.

## 8. Compliance & Policies
Followed Level D AI policy. All prompts, evaluations, and modifications are fully disclosed in `AI_USAGE.md`.

## 9. Checker Results
* Automated Python tests were not present in the starter repository; manual checks were performed successfully.
