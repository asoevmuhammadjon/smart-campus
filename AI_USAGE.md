# AI Usage Disclosure (Level D)

* **Course:** Software Engineering / Requirements Engineering
* **Lab:** Week 03 - Requirements Engineering with AI
* **AI Assistant / Model Used:** OpenAI GPT-4o
* **Date:** September 27, 2026

## Summary of AI Contribution
* **User Stories Generation:** The AI assistant drafted initial user stories based on the fixed Smart Campus study room booking prompt. All generated stories were manually reviewed, refined, and filtered.
* **Acceptance Criteria Drafting:** The AI drafted Given-When-Then criteria for selected user stories, which were subsequently audited and aligned with the system business rules.
* **PlantUML Generation:** PlantUML syntax for use case diagrams was generated by the assistant and validated against actors and use cases.

## Human Review & Refinement
* Every single line produced by the assistant was scrutinized. Redundant stories were removed, priorities were manually assigned based on core booking flows, and traceability mapping was structured through human analysis.
